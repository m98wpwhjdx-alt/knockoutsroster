Golden principles

  1. Stop drugs that increase bleeding, hypotension, aspiration, ketosis, or toxicity risk.
  2. Continue drugs where withdrawal or omission can be more dangerous than surgery.
  3. Ask directly about supplements, vitamins, and natural remedies.
